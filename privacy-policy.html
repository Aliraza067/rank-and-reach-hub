Privacy Policy

What information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Who We Are

Rank and Reach Hub ("we", "us", "our") is an SEO and link building agency founded by Ali Raza. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have. If you have questions, contact us at razaworkspro@gmail.com.

Information We Collect

Information you provide directly: when you submit our contact form, we collect your full name, email address, website URL, service of interest, budget range and any message you write. This information is sent to us via our form processor (Netlify Forms) and to our email inbox so we can respond to your enquiry.

Newsletter signups: if you subscribe to our newsletter, we collect your email address for that purpose only.

Automatically collected information: like most websites, our hosting and analytics tools may automatically log standard technical data such as your IP address, browser type, device type, and pages visited, for security and basic usage analysis.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to enquiries submitted through the contact form
  • To send the newsletter you specifically signed up for
  • To improve this website and understand how it's used
  • To meet legal and security obligations

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information to third parties.

Third-Party Services

This site uses a small number of third-party services that may process data on our behalf: Netlify Forms (to receive and store contact form submissions), Google Fonts (to load web fonts), and WhatsApp/LinkedIn (when you choose to contact us through those channels). Each of these providers has its own privacy policy governing how they handle data.

Cookies

This website uses a limited number of cookies, described in full in our Cookie Policy.

Data Retention

We retain contact form submissions and email correspondence for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your enquiry and maintain business records, after which it may be deleted.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data. To exercise any of these rights, email razaworkspro@gmail.com and we'll respond as soon as reasonably possible.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision note.

Contact Us

For any privacy-related questions, reach us at razaworkspro@gmail.com or WhatsApp.
